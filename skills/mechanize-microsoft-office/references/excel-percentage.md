# Excel percentage formatting and separate popup windows

Observed October 5, 2026 in Excel 16.113.3 through Mechanize MCP and Endly.

The owned sales workbook's G4:G8 margin cells were selected through the observed
Go To dialog. NameBox G4 established the anchor; a fresh capture separately
verified the complete five-cell selection. NameBox alone does not prove range
extent. The enabled Percent Style button received one semantic press, followed
by one exact-window Cmd+S save. Independent XLSX inspection verified built-in
number format 9 (`0%`) on all five cells, every cell value/formula unchanged,
and unchanged chart parts. The original workbook was preserved as a byte-for-byte
copy before formatting. Decimal precision, reopen and unattended range selection
proof remain separate qualification work.

The original formatting postcondition privately compared the Number Format
combo box with `Percentage`. The first helper rejected combo-box comparison,
so the run stopped at a confirmed boundary. A helper-only update added read-only
comparison of known nonsecure combo boxes, retained replacement rejection for
combo boxes, and denied protected labels and unknown subroles. Broker bytes
and Accessibility remained unchanged. Fresh reconciliation confirmed the exact
original predicate at revision 5, and Endly resumed the same run successfully,
skipping the already-confirmed formatting effect. No formatting press was
repeated. Business status remains unverified without an authoritative outcome
adapter; saved-file evidence is recorded separately.

An enabled Format menu button also received one explicit `.showMenu()` action.
The popup appeared as a separate physical window titled Format, with a focused
AXGroup containing menu buttons. Fresh AX evidence and a captured popup verified
its presence. The original postcondition wrongly searched for AXMenu inside the
workbook window and remains unknown. Do not infer popup absence from a workbook
capture; capture discovery can expose a separate popup. Changing app focus
dismissed this popup without replaying the showMenu action or editing the file.

Paste was disabled while the chart was selected and the clipboard was empty.
Its precondition stopped the proposed showMenu operation before input. Do not
remove the positive enabled-state guard. These cases do not qualify general
menu handling, embedded PowerPoint charts or complete unattended Office work.
